Gym Safety Precautions

General Safety Guidelines

Essential Information
Warm-up and Cool-down
Always perform proper warm-up exercises before starting your workout and cool-down stretches afterward. This helps prevent injuries and improves flexibility.
Proper Form
Maintain correct form during all exercises. If unsure about proper technique, ask a trainer for guidance. Poor form can lead to serious injuries.
Hydration
Stay hydrated before, during, and after your workout. Bring a water bottle and take regular sips to maintain optimal performance.

Equipment Safety

Proper Usage
Machine Setup
Always adjust machines to your body size and strength level. Check that all pins and settings are secure before use.
Weight Selection
Start with lighter weights and gradually increase as you build strength. Never attempt weights beyond your capability.
Equipment Maintenance
Wipe down equipment after use and report any malfunctioning equipment to staff immediately.

Health & Wellness

Well-being
Medical Clearance
Consult with your doctor before starting any new exercise program, especially if you have pre-existing medical conditions.
Rest and Recovery
Allow adequate rest between workouts and ensure you get enough sleep for proper muscle recovery.
Nutrition
Maintain a balanced diet and proper nutrition to support your workout routine and recovery.
